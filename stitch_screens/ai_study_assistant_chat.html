Absolutely! The Schrödinger equation is the fundamental law of motion in quantum mechanics. Here are the core pillars you'll need for your exam:

  • Wave Function (ψ): It describes the quantum state of a system and contains all the information we can know about it.
  • Probability Density: The square of the wave function's magnitude gives us the probability of finding a particle at a specific point.
  • Energy Quantization: Solving the equation often yields discrete "allowed" energy levels, explaining why atoms are stable.
