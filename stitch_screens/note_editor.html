Complexity theory is a central topic in theoretical computer science. It focuses on classifying computational problems according to their inherent difficulty.

P vs NP Problem

The P versus NP problem is a major unsolved problem in computer science. It asks whether every problem whose solution can be quickly verified can also be solved quickly.
