Physics 101: Thermodynamics

The First Law of Thermodynamics states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another. In any isolated system, the total energy remains constant.

ΔU = Q - W

Where:
ΔU = change in internal energy
Q = heat added to system
W = work done by system
