QUESTION

What is the primary function of the Myelin Sheath in neural transmission?

Tap to reveal answer

ANSWER

The Myelin Sheath acts as an insulating layer that forms around nerves. Its primary purpose is to allow electrical impulses to transmit quickly and efficiently along the nerve cells. It enables saltatory conduction, significantly increasing signal speed.
